One sequence, belief by belief

The same sequence is shown to two networks; only the open token differs. Follow any bar: at its left end every belief returns to its starting value, and just past its right end the columns repeat what the enclosing bracket held just before the bar began. 'Probe' rows are linear readouts of the last block, fitted on other sequences. 'Close odds' needs no probe: it is the network's own probability for each close token, renormalised, and exists only inside brackets.
