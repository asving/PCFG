One sequence, belief by belief

The same sequence is shown to two networks; only the open token differs. At every open the three state points jump to the centre of their triangle (the starting belief); the type point jumps to the centre when the open is #, and straight to a corner when the open names the type. Just past every close, all four points jump back to where they were in the last triangle before that bracket opened. Bars above the tokens mark each bracket from its open to its close.
