How do the thread-reading heads count recency? Take a position i whose two most recent own tokens are
i and i-1, and whose third is j3. Share = attention on j3 / (attention on i-1 + attention on j3).
Counted in own tokens, a child between j3 and i-1 changes nothing: the share equals the reference.
Counted in positions, a head whose attention decays with distance would drop the share towards 0.
